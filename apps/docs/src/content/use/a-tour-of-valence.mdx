Valence is a streaming service you run yourself. Once it is installed and pointed at your media, the people in your household sign in from a browser, the desktop app, a phone or a television, choose their face, and find everything the server holds in one place: films, programmes, music, books and audiobooks. This page is a walk through what they see. If you are setting a server up, start with [First run](/use/first-run) and [Libraries](/use/libraries).

## Choosing who is watching

Signing in leads to a wall of faces, one for each profile in the household. Each profile keeps its own history, progress, favourites, ratings and hidden titles, so one person's half-watched series is never in the way of another's. Profiles, and the limits an administrator can set on what an account may see, are in [Accounts and roles](/use/accounts-and-roles#profiles-and-the-household).

## The home page

The home page opens on a few featured titles, one after another, across the top. Beneath them are rows:

- **Continue watching**, for anything part way through, with the next episode of a programme in place of the one just finished.
- **Coming up**, for programmes in the library with an episode still to air, saying which and when.
- **Recently added**, newest first.
- Rows by genre, such as "New in Drama", which keep arriving for as long as you scroll.

Each title in a row is its backdrop with the title's own logo drawn into it. A thin bar beneath shows how far through it you are. The handful of titles that arrived most recently carry a flag along the foot of the picture: **Recently added**, or **New episode** for a programme that was already there when its latest episode arrived.

Rest the pointer on a title and it grows into a preview, playing a moment from it, with the button to play or resume and the buttons to start again, keep it and hide it. Where there is not room for them all, the rest fold into a menu. Rows turn a whole card at a time, with the arrows beside their titles or a swipe.

## A title's page

Pressing a title opens its page. Beneath the name are its facts, such as the year, running time and rating. Then comes its certificate, drawn in the colours and shape of the board that issued it, and badges for how it looks and sounds: **4K**, **HD** or **SD**, the dynamic range such as **HDR10** or **Dolby Vision**, and the soundtrack, such as **Dolby Atmos** or **5.1**. Which certificate is shown follows the region set in the server's settings.

The page also holds the synopsis, genres, cast, trailer, extras, other versions of the same film, and every action there is: play, keep, rate, download, share, mark watched and hide. A programme's page lists its seasons and episodes, and says how many are left to watch.

## Search

Search looks through every library at once, and can be narrowed to films, programmes, music or books. For somebody who may ask for things, it also looks through the catalogue for what the server does not have yet, and asking for it is one press away. See [Requests](/use/requests).

## Watching

The player plays the file as it is wherever the device can take it, and otherwise transcodes it on the fly. Its controls hold quality, audio tracks, subtitles and their timing, speed, and a button to skip an intro, a recap or the credits. Pause and leave it, and after a few seconds with nothing touched the picture dims and says what you are watching: the programme, the season, the episode and what happens in it. It stays until you move the pointer or press a key. Everything the player does is in [Playing media](/use/playing-media).

## Music, books and audiobooks

Music has a player bar that follows you from page to page, a queue, lyrics in time with the song, and playlists. Books open in a reader of their own, and audiobooks in a player in the corner of the page. See [Playing media](/use/playing-media#music), [Reading books](/use/playing-media#reading-books) and [Audiobooks](/use/audiobooks).

## Together and elsewhere

A watch party keeps everybody at the same moment of the same film. A share link hands one title or one programme to somebody with no account at all. A download takes a film to a phone to watch with no server. See [Watch parties](/use/playing-media#watch-parties), [Sharing](/use/sharing) and [The phone app](/use/the-phone-app).

## The account menu

Your face in the top corner opens the account menu: your profile, your downloads, the theme (light, dark or the system's), how much things move, and the way out. Administrators also find the admin area here, covered from [Settings](/use/settings) onwards.
