Audiobooks live in a book library alongside ebooks and comics. They are scanned, matched and found like any other book, and they play in a player of their own that stays in the corner of the page while you browse.

## Filing them

Put audiobooks in a library of the **Books** type. Valence reads them as sound when a file is one of these:

| Format       | Extensions             |
| ------------ | ---------------------- |
| MPEG-4 audio | `.m4b`, `.m4a`, `.aac` |
| MP3          | `.mp3`                 |
| Ogg          | `.ogg`, `.opus`        |
| FLAC         | `.flac`                |

A book can be one file holding the whole thing, which is what an `.m4b` usually is, or a folder of tracks, one per part. Chapters come from the marks the file carries inside itself where it has them, and otherwise each track is a chapter.

What the file's tags say wins over its name. The album is the book, with a shop's "(Unabridged)" left off; the artist who made the album is its author; the description or comment is its blurb; and the picture inside the file, or a cover image in its folder, is its cover. Files named the way most audiobook managers name them, `Author/Series/1 - Title`, are read the same way as ebooks. See [Libraries](/use/libraries#books) for how books are named.

A book that exists as both an ebook and an audiobook sits on the shelf once, with both inside it. Its page offers reading and listening side by side, and a book you can only listen to leads with listening.

## Listening

Press the listening button on a book's page and the player appears in the corner, showing the cover, the chapter playing, and buttons to go back 15 seconds, play or pause, and go on 30 seconds. It sits apart from the music player, and the two take turns: starting one pauses the other.

Open the player for everything else:

- How far through the whole book you are, with a handle to drag. The book moves when you let go.
- The chapter before and after, and the full list of chapters to go straight to.
- **Speed**: 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× or 2×.
- **Sleep timer**: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of the chapter.

Closing the player keeps your place. The home of the books library shows **Continue listening**, most recent first, with the chapter you are on and how long is left.

## On other devices

Where you have got to belongs to your profile, not to the device, so a book started in the browser carries on from the same moment on the phone or the desktop app. The phone app has the same player, with the same speeds and sleep timer. See [The phone app](/use/the-phone-app).
